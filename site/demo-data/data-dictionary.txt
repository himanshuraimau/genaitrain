KITE & CO: DEMO DATA (made up, for teaching only)
Kite & Co is an imaginary online stationery shop in India. Every name, email and number is invented.
Period: 7 Aug 2026 to 4 Nov 2026 (90 days). Money is in Indian rupees (INR).

FILES
orders.csv    From the shop system. One row per order line.
signups.csv   From website analytics. One row per day per channel.
ad_spend.csv  From the marketing team. One row per day per paid channel (google, instagram).
kiteco.xlsx   The same three tables as three sheets in one Excel workbook.

COLUMNS
order_id         Unique order number. (A few appear twice by mistake.)
order_date       Day of the order. Written in three styles: 2026-10-06, 06/10/2026 (day first), 6 Oct 2026.
customer_id      Customer code. TEST means an internal test order.
customer_email   Fake address on example.com.
country          Where it shipped. Spelled in several ways.
channel          Where the customer came from. Spelled in several ways (Instagram, insta, IG ...).
product          Product name.
category         Product group. Sometimes blank.
quantity         Units bought. A negative number means a return was typed in by mistake or by design.
unit_price_inr   List price of one unit, before any discount.
discount_code    SAVE20 = 20% off. WELCOME10 = 10% off the first order. Blank = no discount.
status           Delivered, Shipped, Cancelled or Refunded, in mixed capital letters.
date, channel    In signups.csv and ad_spend.csv: clean day and channel names (google, instagram, newsletter, referral, direct).
visits, signups  Website visits and new accounts created.
spend_inr, clicks  Money spent on ads and clicks received.

KNOWN MESS (on purpose, for the cleaning step)
Mixed date styles, many spellings of country and channel, repeated orders, test orders, negative quantities,
one or two prices with extra zeros, blank categories, mixed-case status.

LIVE VERSION
The same data keeps growing every day at /api/shop?table=orders (or signups, ad_spend).
In Google Sheets:  =IMPORTDATA("https://genai-notebook.vercel.app/api/shop?table=signups")
