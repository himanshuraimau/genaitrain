A suggested ladder

Which one first?

  1. Brand new: Google AI Essentials or OpenAI AI Foundations, depending on which tool you use at work.
  2. Want the thinking skills: Anthropic's AI Fluency. It's free and works with any assistant.
  3. Want to build skills: this notebook's Chapters 4 to 5 and 10, then Anthropic's Introduction to Agent Skills.
  4. Want depth and code: DeepLearning.AI's Agent Skills with Anthropic.

These are suggestions based on each course's own description. I haven't taken any of them.

What we learned from the courses

Ideas worth borrowing for your own sessions

Teach by job task

Google's course is organized by what people do: brainstorm, research, write, analyze. Use that for your examples.

Teach checking, not just asking

Both Anthropic's AI Fluency and Google cover verifying output and responsible use. That's Chapter 11 here.

Skills vs everything else

Anthropic's skills courses spend a lesson on "skills vs other features". That's Chapter 3 here.

Don't skip sharing and troubleshooting

Anthropic's course has lessons on both. Chapter 10 has a troubleshooting table; sharing across a team is a good next chapter.
