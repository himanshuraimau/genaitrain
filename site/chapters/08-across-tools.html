Different names and menus. The same idea underneath.

The big picture

Claude

Claude skills

  • On Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, with code execution turned on.
  • Create: chat with skill-creator, record your screen (Mac desktop, Pro/Max/Team), or upload a ZIP.
  • Skills don't sync across places. claude.ai, the developer API and Claude Code are separate libraries. Upload to each.
  • In claude.ai a skill belongs to you. It isn't shared org-wide, and admins can't manage it centrally.

Anthropic: Agent Skills overview · Creating custom skills

ChatGPT

ChatGPT skills

  • Find them under Plugins → Skills tab. Create by chat, in the editor, or by uploading a file. A built-in skill-creator helps.
  • Uploaded skills are scanned: Ready, Needs Review, or Blocked.
  • Share from the skill's menu or publish to a workspace library. Admins control who can create, upload and share.
  • In Codex (OpenAI's coding tool) skills load from folders on your computer.

Can't see Skills? OpenAI's help page lists Business, Enterprise, Healthcare and Edu as eligible. One outside report says consumer plans got a beta in Oct 2026. It's likely your plan or admin settings, not you. Check your account.

Gemini

Gemini: skills are replacing Gems

What skills are

  • Reusable instructions. Run with / or let Gemini pick.
  • They work inline in the same chat. Stack several in one task.
  • Can include reference files (text, PDFs, images).
  • Gemini can draft one from your chats.

Dates to know

  • Nov 2026: Gems support ends for personal accounts
  • Mar 2027: business and enterprise Workspace
  • Jun 2027: education Workspace
  • Remaining Gems are migrated to skills automatically.

Skills in the Gemini app and in Google Workspace are separate: make them in each place. Sharing and Drive file support were announced as "coming".

Microsoft 365 Copilot

  • Skills are arriving in Copilot Chat (rollout Oct to mid-Nov 2026) and in declarative agents.
  • Same shape: a folder with SKILL.md. Users can create, upload and edit. Admins can publish, install or block.

Take-away

Learn the idea once

The words change: Skill, Gem, Custom GPT, Project.
"Save the method, load it when needed" stays the same.
Because SKILL.md is a shared format, a skill from one tool can often be copied to another.
