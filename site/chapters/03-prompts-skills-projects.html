Chapter 3

Prompt, skill, or project?

Which tool to reach for, and when.

Three words, three jobs

One line each

Prompt
What you type right now. One-off. Gone when the chat ends.
Skill
A saved method. Loaded only when the task matches. Works across your chats.
Project
A workspace with files and instructions for one topic, always on inside it.

Two more you'll hear about:

  • Custom instructions: always-on rules about you ("keep answers short"). Apply to every chat.
  • Connectors: permission for the AI to reach into Gmail, Drive, Notion and similar.

How to choose

A quick decision guide

Doing it once?

→ Prompt.

Same files across many chats on one topic (a client, a course)?

→ Project.

Same procedure again and again (format, steps, tone)?

→ Skill.

Same preference everywhere?

→ Custom instructions.

Best combo: a connector gives access, a skill gives the method, a project gives context, and your prompt gives today's request.

Worked example

Monthly report for a client

  1. Project "Acme Corp": holds their brief, past reports, contact notes.
  2. Skill "monthly-report": your fixed layout, tone, and checks.
  3. Connector to your spreadsheet app so the AI can read the numbers.
  4. Prompt "Make this month's report, highlight anything over budget."
Each piece does one job. That's why the result is both consistent and specific.
