What they all do

Six patterns to copy

  1. Start with a task you repeat weekly: release notes, a daily briefing, interview summaries.
  2. Feed it examples of "great": Rekhi's last 20 release notes, his saved frameworks.
  3. Treat the skill as a living document. Moretti edited his about 50 times.
  4. Keep a human in the loop. Rekhi hand-verifies pricing, Moretti reviews tickets.
  5. Fix the output shape (template, report, document) so results are predictable.
  6. Add access (browser, database, files) and the wins get bigger.
