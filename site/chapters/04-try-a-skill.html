Chapter 4 · Activity

Make something with a built-in skill

No setup. Ask for a deck, a sheet, or a document and see a skill at work.

What's already there

Skills you get for free

Slides

PowerPoint decks

Sheets

Excel files with formulas and charts

Docs

Word documents

PDFs

Formatted reports

  • Claude: these four document skills switch on by themselves when you ask for those files. File creation / code execution must be on in Settings.
  • ChatGPT: eligible accounts include a built-in skill-creator skill (used to make new skills).
  • Gemini: run a skill with / then its name, or let Gemini pick one.

Pick one (15 minutes)

Activity menu

A. Deck in 5 min
"Create a 6-slide PowerPoint explaining my job to a new joiner. One idea per slide, with speaker notes."
B. Messy to clean
Paste a messy list (expenses, attendance). "Create an Excel sheet with totals and a chart."
C. One-page brief
Paste rough notes. "Turn these into a one-page PDF summary with headings."

Use the five-part recipe from Chapter 1: context, task, examples, constraints, output format.

Steps

Run it

  1. Open your AI assistant and start a new chat.
  2. Write your request with context, task, constraints and format.
  3. Wait for the file. Download it and open it.
  4. Reply with one change ("make slide 3 less wordy", "add a total row").
  5. Note what the skill got right without being told.

Debrief

Talk about it

  • What did you ask for, and what came back?
  • What did the AI do without being told (layout, fonts, structure)?
  • What would you want it to do your way every time? That's a skill idea.
Hold on to that last answer. It becomes your own skill in the next chapter.
